# Literature and scientific context

The contributions are stated against specific equations and theorem hypotheses. The correspondence below separates the external inputs, the questions addressed here, and the remaining problems. [Bibliography](bibliography.md) collects the manuscript references; the linked research surveys retain the more detailed comparisons.

## Negative energy, coherent motion, and dispersion

| Primary source | Relation to this work |
|---|---|
| Boulenger–Lenzmann, *Blowup for Biharmonic NLS*, Ann. Sci. ENS 50 (2017), 503–544. [Paper](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.24033/asens.2326.pdf) | Radial negative-energy blowup/growup and the question of removing radiality. The excited-wave construction gives an obstruction to an unconditional nonradial extension; it does not contradict their radial theorems. |
| Dinh, *Finite-Time Blow-Up of Radial Negative-Energy Solutions for the Mass-Critical Biharmonic Nonlinear Schrödinger Equation*, 2 October 2026. [Primary manuscript page](https://ai.meta.com/research/publications/finite-time-blow-up-of-radial-negative-energy-solutions-for-the-mass-critical-biharmonic-nonlinear-schrodinger-equation/) | A recent radial finite-time theorem and exponentially localized virial mechanism. The preliminary notes test the extra interpolation needed outside radial symmetry. The later global waves settle the unrestricted negative-energy implication negatively. |
| Gou, *Blowup of cylindrically symmetric solutions for biharmonic NLS*. [arXiv v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.08167v3), [journal](https://doi.org/10.1017/S001309152400066X) | The explicit comparison concerns Theorem 1.2 in the dated arXiv version. The localized axial-moment calculation and constructed wave are given together. This repository is not an author or journal erratum. |
| Pausader–Shao, *The mass-critical fourth-order Schrödinger equation in high dimensions*. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/0906.1547) | Compact-orbit virial and mass-current identities in Section 6 are antecedents for the moving-center and recurrent-profile discussion. The later quantitative statements use their own stated regularity and confinement hypotheses. |
| Bugiera–Lenzmann–Schikorra–Sok, *On symmetry of traveling solitary waves for dispersion generalized NLS*. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.06771) | Existing traveling-wave variational and symmetry theory. The present excited-wave construction and uniform near-critical exclusion concern a different combination of energy, response sign, and mass. |
| Lin–Zeng, *Instability, index theorem, and exponential trichotomy for linear Hamiltonian PDEs*. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.04016) | Hamiltonian index and trichotomy input for the certified excited wave. The certificate supplies the concrete Morse, kernel, and response data needed for its application. |
| Côte–Le Coz, *High-speed excited multi-solitons in nonlinear Schrödinger equations*. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1007.3034) | Predecessor for excited multiwave gluing. The zero-momentum argument uses a corrected quadratic form and finite-rank directions at the small speeds of the supplied profiles. |
| Fernández–Jeanjean–Mandel–Mariș, *Some non-homogeneous Gagliardo–Nirenberg inequalities and application to a biharmonic non-linear Schrödinger equation*. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.01448) | Mixed-dispersion inequalities and normalized variational theory. Dilation conditions and two-threshold examples in this repository specify which profile gaps survive a dispersion change. |
| Fibich–Ilan–Schochet, *Critical exponents and collapse of nonlinear Schrödinger equations with anisotropic fourth-order dispersion*. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1088/0951-7715/16/5/314) | Physical and scaling antecedent for anisotropic quadratic/quartic optics. The new mixed-model statement is a profile exclusion, with existence and physical preparation still unresolved. |

## Occupations, critical cores, and collapse scale

| Primary source | Relation to this work |
|---|---|
| Dolbeault–Felmer–Loss–Paturel, *Lieb–Thirring type inequalities and Gagliardo–Nirenberg inequalities for systems*. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0506052) | Occupation numbers and kinetic/spectral duality are inherited structures. The separated-largest-occupation theorem gives a sharp scalar right side in a specified two-dimensional regime. |
| Hong–Kwon–Yoon, *Global existence versus finite time blowup dichotomy for the system of nonlinear Schrödinger equations*. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.06811) | Common-density systems, variational thresholds, and the global/blowup framework. Exact fixed-Gram constructions and critical-core decompositions are compared with this framework. |
| Carlen–Frank–Lieb, *Stability estimates for the lowest eigenvalue of a Schrödinger operator*. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.5032) | Published scalar stability is an input, not a new theorem here. Combining it with the Townes homogeneous excited-space gap gives a weighted spectral remainder. |
| Bourgain–Wang, *Construction of blowup solutions for the nonlinear Schrödinger equation with critical nonlinearity*. [Paper](https://numdam.org/item/ASNSP_1997_4_25_1-2_197_0/) | Source of the pseudo-conformal core with a flat regular tail. The vector construction preserves a specified full Gram matrix; it does not claim a new scalar blowup rate. |
| Read–Schulz, *The sharp one-dimensional Lieb–Thirring inequality for the sum of eigenvalues*, arXiv:2609.10478v2. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10478v2) | External sharp one-dimensional and operator-valued input for the quintic occupation threshold. The additional core/background and positive-deficit deductions are identified separately. |
| Raphaël–Szeftel, *Existence and uniqueness of minimal blow-up solutions to an inhomogeneous mass critical NLS*. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1001.1627) | Minimal-mass concentration and interaction with an inhomogeneous medium. The present potential theorem makes the time-integrated curvature condition and gradient-normalized scale explicit. |
| Schlag, *Dispersive estimates for Schrödinger operators in dimension two*. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0405437) | Static linear dispersive input after the Townes threshold obstruction is excluded. Static decay alone does not prove the nonlinear moving-core estimates. |

## Collective binding and the two-dimensional sharp constant

| Primary source | Relation to this work |
|---|---|
| Frank–Gontier–Lewin, *The nonlinear Schrödinger equation for orthonormal functions II: Application to Lieb–Thirring inequalities*. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.04964) | Finite-rank duality and strict binding for $\gamma>\max(0,2-d/2)$. At $d=2$, the strict inequality excludes the endpoint $\gamma=1$ studied here. |
| Frank–Gontier–Lewin, *Optimizers for the finite-rank Lieb–Thirring inequality*. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.05984) | Optimizer existence, Euler–Lagrange systems and concentration compactness. Their hypotheses are retained when using the finite-rank reduction. |
| Frank–Gontier–Lewin, *The periodic Lieb–Thirring inequality*. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.02981) | Equality of the periodic and whole-space best constants motivates the full Brillouin-zone searches. A finite numerical search does not determine that best constant. |
| Levitt, *Best constants in Lieb–Thirring inequalities: a numerical investigation*. [Paper](https://ems.press/journals/jst/articles/12471) | Numerical optimization and splitting phenomena are established precedents. Hermite and periodic calculations here are concrete searches, with recorded convergence and refinement data. |
| *Sharp one-dimensional Lieb–Thirring inequalities for matrix potentials*, 5 October 2026. [Source cited in the derivation](https://www.emergentmind.com/openai-math-explorer/papers/526.pdf?v=adc7f12) | The resolvent/action construction is an explicitly attributed external input. The matrix commutator remainder and density-transfer deductions are stated separately in the linked proof notes. The source's attribution is retained. |

The two-dimensional exponent-one target remains unresolved in this repository. The arbitrary-rank remainders and full-response identities are separate proved statements; the separated-core asymptotics and searches address restricted configurations.
