# Negative-Energy Global Solutions and Traveling-Wave Mass Gaps in Mass-Critical Dispersive Equations

Shuxing Yang

**质量临界色散方程中的负能量全局解与行波质量间隙**

[English abstract](abstract.md) · [中文摘要](abstract.zh-CN.md) · [LaTeX](title-and-abstract.tex) · [Proof sequence](PROOFS.md)

The paper addresses how negative-energy coherent motion can persist globally and why its traveling profiles remain separated from the scalar critical mass. Its organizing mechanisms are excited-state phase response, exact momentum cancellation, and near-optimizer phase coercivity.

The supporting manuscripts and derivations are organized in the proof sequence below. Occupation-dependent second-order systems and the unrestricted orthogonal-function problem are related developments in the repository; their conclusions are not silently imported into the biharmonic theorem.
