# Relationship to prior work

The archive has two specific external targets. It should not be described as having solved many unspecified classical conjectures.

1. **Boulenger–Lenzmann, _Blowup for biharmonic NLS_**, Annales scientifiques de l'École Normale Supérieure 50(3), 503–544 (2017), DOI [10.24033/asens.2326](https://doi.org/10.24033/asens.2326). Section 1.5 discusses removing radial symmetry. The project constructs nonradial negative-energy solutions with bounded H² norm, so the negative-energy blowup/growup conclusion cannot be extended unconditionally to all nonradial data. The published radial theorem is not contradicted. A proposed removal of an assumption is not automatically a separately stated formal conjecture.

2. **Gou, _Blowup of cylindrically symmetric solutions for biharmonic NLS_**, [arXiv:2205.08167](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.08167), specifically the v3 Theorem 1.2 formulation compared in the research archive. `BNLS_Conflict_Proof_and_Verification_20261007` records the exact hypothesis comparison and the axial-moment issue. The claim of a counterexample rests on the constructed solution satisfying those hypotheses, not merely on identifying a proof gap. No author response, accepted correction, or journal adjudication is asserted here.

The mass-gap theorem, sharp fixed-core radiation infimum, quantitative residual theorem, mixed-dispersion extension, and subsequent transport/spectral analysis are further questions addressed by this project. Their exact scope is in [the results index](results.md). They must not each be relabeled as a previously named conjecture without a matching source.

## Logical distinction

- A proof gap identifies where an argument needs an additional estimate.
- A counterexample supplies a solution contradicting a precisely quantified statement.
- The archived N3/N4 constructions supply the latter mathematical mechanism, conditional on the validity of their analytic proof and certified inputs as any research manuscript is.
- The first-class work does not establish a complete classification of all near-critical trajectories.

Primary source PDF for Boulenger–Lenzmann: <https://www.numdam.org/item/10.24033/asens.2326.pdf>. The frozen comparison in the archive specifies the Gou version; later versions must be compared separately.
