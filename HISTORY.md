## Formulations corrected by the development

- The original unconditional nonradial negative-energy collapse target is ruled out by the N3/N4 excited-wave constructions. What remains open is a specifically near-critical statement for general trajectories.
- Earlier conditional gluing notes are superseded by the finite-rank corrected-energy construction. The final proof does not require the former SC assumption.
- The early unstable-dimension lower bound is superseded by the certified radial quartet and the sufficiently small-speed count of 31.
- R15's small total secondary occupation is strengthened by R16's bound on the second-to-first occupation ratio. Neither is the unrestricted 2D occupation theorem.
- A phase-residual obstruction to profiles is not a classification of recurrent time-dependent motion.
- Positive small dispersion perturbations do not universally preserve a mass gap; the dilation hypothesis is essential.
- Static concentration scales, actual temporal collapse rates, and a formal matched candidate are distinct conclusions.
- The one-dimensional sharp Lieb–Thirring theorem belongs to Read–Schulz. Its use in the quintic model is recorded as an external input.
- Numerical splitting of distant cores has predecessors; repeated local convergence to split states does not prove the global sharp inequality.

## Initial problem survey

The preliminary discussion also considered exact collapse rates, soliton resolution, FPUT thermalization and anomalous transport, Anderson localization, spin-chain dynamics, Vlasov–Maxwell, Prandtl, Boussinesq, wave kinetics, and many-body localization. These served as problem-selection context. No separate completed theorem in those models is represented in this repository without a corresponding source derivation. The retained research developed the critical focusing equations described above.
