## What the proofs establish

The strongest actual-flow statement is the boundedness of all critical-mass solutions with energy strictly below the computed concentration level, assuming conservative local flow. A separate proof establishes that flow for the stated quartic symbols in dimensions one through three. The quantitative polarization inequality is unconditional in its stated scalar-dispersion, total-density classes and permits infinitely many components.

The small-velocity spectral theorem retains the explicit operator, branch and nondegeneracy hypotheses. Linear spectral or secular growth is not silently promoted to nonlinear instability. The transport theorem applies to strong, smoothly approximable energy solutions with a uniform energy-space bound. It does not rule out every form of persistent exterior activity.
