THEOREMS
1.1: Fixed N and order m>1, real-even positive elliptic homogeneous symbol:
     every negative-energy weak traveling profile has mass at least Mc,a+epsilon.
1.8: This gap remains uniformly positive throughout a sufficiently small
     relative C0 neighborhood of a fixed angular symbol. No uniform
     derivative bounds or compactness of the neighborhood are required.
2.1: Prescribed radiation mass and momentum: exact free-energy infimum is
     a**(momentum)/(2 mass^(m-1)); it is not attained, but every greater
     energy is realized exactly by Schwartz data.
2.6-2.7: The profile gap passes to the stated finite-core/dispersive-remainder
     decompositions, including decomposition only along a time sequence.
3.1-3.2: For isotropic fourth-order dispersion with a nonnegative second-order
     term, mass tightness plus bounded first kinetic time averages forces
     counter-momentum center drift. Failure of the required drift forces
     divergence of full initial-time kinetic averages.
