# Open problems

## Negative-energy dynamics

1. **Near-critical general trajectories.** Does a uniform mass neighborhood above Mc exclude every zero-momentum, negative-energy, forward-global, uniformly H²-bounded biharmonic solution? The profile residual theorem does not rule out recurrent deformation.
2. **Minimum traveling-wave mass.** Decide whether the negative-energy profile mass infimum is attained at negative energy or approached through the zero-energy compactification alternative.
3. **Mixed-model existence.** Construct and certify a negative-intrinsic-energy traveling wave for one quadratic and two quartic directions. The uniform exclusion theorem is already established independently of existence.

## Occupation thresholds and collapse

4. **Unrestricted two-dimensional occupations.** Prove the sharp orthogonal GN/Lieb–Thirring inequality for arbitrary finite rank, or construct a certified violating family. This would determine the unrestricted occupation threshold.
5. **Rough-background core scale.** Classify the limiting core width without the additional curvature control. Static transverse dispersive decay does not provide the needed estimate along indefinitely shrinking coupled cores.
6. **Quadratic quintic collapse.** Turn the exact-Gram matched energy family and inner/outer expansions into a genuine solution with λ(t) proportional to (T−t)².
7. **Scattering without finite variance.** Extend the stated all-time scattering/core conclusions to the appropriate general energy-space data.

## Spectral and physical completion

8. **Ground-wave spectral assumptions.** Establish the real-sector nondegeneracy needed by the strongest spectral and nonlinear departure results in the specified biharmonic models.
9. **Physical realization.** Convert a certified mixed-model branch into a feasible preparation with controlled dispersion, aperture, loss, and nonlinearity. No such experimental realization is claimed by the current continuation calculations.

These questions are attached to existing results in [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md). They are not prerequisites for stating the completed constructions, profile gaps, restricted occupation thresholds, or scattering identities.
