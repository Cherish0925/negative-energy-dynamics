Main statements
1. Theorem 1.1: uniform negative-energy traveling-profile mass gap over the full one-sided positive dispersion and nonlinear-energy addition classes. The shifted-frequency identity is equation (18).
2. Theorem 2.1: arbitrarily small, same-order nonhomogeneous form perturbations can remove that gap. Their full sharp GN critical mass remains the principal critical mass. These are modified equations, not the pure homogeneous equation.
3. Theorem 3.3: positive inertia with a quantitative residual error. Theorem 3.6 and Corollary 3.7 constrain shape evolution of sufficiently regular relative-periodic solutions.
4. Appendix A supplies the variational foundations. Appendix B gives the explicit static interaction-virial counterexample. Appendix C gives an arbitrary-order comparison with established mixed-dispersion variational mechanisms.

Scientific status
The manuscript contains the analytic proofs. The mass-gap constants are qualitative. This installment does not prove an unrestricted dynamical mass gap or classify all nonradial solutions. Numerical files from earlier installments are not dependencies.

The reviews directory preserves the internal mathematical and literature checks. The manuscript is the definitive statement; exploratory chronological notes and superseded variants are deliberately omitted.
