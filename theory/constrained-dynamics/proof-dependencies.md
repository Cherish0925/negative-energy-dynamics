# Proof dependencies

The amplification argument uses the following identities and inputs:
- Critical scaling normalizes sup A without changing d or zero momentum.
- Quantitative constant-phase control is a real O(2) result, not the vacuous
  complex single-component polarization statement.
- Slow-center speed is independent of the possibly large concentration radius.
- Uniform exterior mass uses negative-energy splitting, not assumed tightness.
- Exterior virial kinetic density is the full Hessian, not pointwise Laplacian.
- Disjoint moving annuli and fixed-radius ordered limits remove transition error.
- The amplitude family is normalized with M(Q)=Mc.
- Nonlinear unstable orbit conserves exact M,P,E by backward convergence.
- N=3 uses C^(1,2/3) H2 remainder; real L+ nondegeneracy remains explicit.
- Internal correlation conservation is known machinery and is credited.

Related literature: BL, BCGJ, Lenzmann-Sok, Hong-Kwon-Yoon,
and the Dinh-Muse primary manuscript; comparison does not claim first priority.
