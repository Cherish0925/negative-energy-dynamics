# Symmetry, fixed-point signs, and parameter ranges

The three-dimensional continuation uses the real-linear operator
$L=L_+\oplus L_-$. Its fixed-point term is
$-vL^{-1}(i\partial_1w)$, rather than $-ivL^{-1}(\partial_1w)$.
The norm estimates use the former expression.

The radial quartet certificate gives a Rouché bound below 0.365771,
including the positive infinite-dimensional complement and the transfer
to the exact profile. The resulting stable and unstable real dimensions
are 31 for sufficiently small nonzero speed.

The 31-parameter reflected family has inversion symmetry and finite
variance. Restricting every parameter to transverse O(3) symmetry does
not retain that full dimension. A cylindrically symmetric zero-momentum
example follows separately from the equivariant contraction.

The three-dimensional explicit interval is $0<|v|\le10^{-9}$.
The 31-dimensional assertion uses an unspecified sufficiently small
speed in four dimensions; these are different parameter statements.
